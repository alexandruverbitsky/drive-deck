Privacy

Drive Deck Lite has no accounts, database, AI provider keys or application backend. Published station lists and favorites are public JSON files.

On this device

Your theme, volume, ticker visibility and last selected station are stored in browser local storage. They do not sync between devices. Clear this website’s data in your browser to remove them. Radio does not automatically start on reload.

Other providers

Radio streams connect directly to their broadcasters. Website shortcuts open their own sites. The optional TradingView ticker receives normal connection information and may use cookies or analytics under its policies. Pausing or hiding the ticker unloads it. Your hosting provider, including GitHub Pages, can log requests under its own privacy policy.

Drive Deck Lite does not add its own analytics. Live audio and markets require an internet connection.
